# 2.1.0 — curated product and uniqueness restoration

Restored ΔMYCN, MYCNOT and MUSEP/MNOP, their original colours, and N-Myc as named products. The products were already represented by valid sequences among the 42 current ORFs; loss of curated name mapping and replacement by generic evidence colours caused the visibility regression.

Restored explicit unique-feature display and preserved shared features. Singleton membership was present in v2.0.0 data; the correction adds an accessible all/unique/shared panel, counts and scoped exports rather than changing valid carrier assignments. There are 11 singleton and 31 shared current ORF models.

Separated transcript structures, ORFs, curated products, sequence relationships and evidence. Preserved all seven GENCODE v50 models and reviewed RefSeq MYCN-XM. Product identity, literature detection, predicted sequence relationships and unresolved function are distinguished. MUSEP/MNOP nomenclature remains project curation with its published sequence-identification limit visible.

The additive product/relationship model uses schema 2.1.0 and reference build mycn-2. v2.0.0 and the historical mixed-reference application remain preserved. No historical sample abundance or unverified peptide attribution has been introduced.
