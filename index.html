    Reference and evidence

    The default displays the complete current GENCODE MYCN transcript set plus the reviewed RefSeq MYCN transcript variant 4, shown as MYCN-XM (TV4). MYCN-XM is a study display alias, not a GENCODE transcript name.

    TV1–TV6, TV2+, and NC are laboratory aliases used by our laboratory. Current GENCODE and RefSeq identifiers remain authoritative.

    ORFs are independently enumerated from the current official transcript sequences: every AUG start to the first in-frame standard-code stop, including nested starts, with a minimum of 8 amino acids. Non-AUG starts and no-stop ORFs are outside this enumeration. ORF numbers are specific to this build and do not map to the former 56-ORF viewer.

    Annotated CDS: DATABASE_ANNOTATED. Other ORFs and physicochemical properties: COMPUTATIONAL_PREDICTION. Domain coordinates: DATABASE_ANNOTATED, shown only after an exact protein-sequence match to the cited UniProt record. No experimental expression, translation, stability, conservation or functional claim is assigned to a predicted ORF.

    For research and educational use; this is not a clinical diagnostic tool. Annotation and laboratory nomenclature do not establish sample-specific expression or function. Historical laboratory MS, ribosome-profiling and conservation assertions have been removed from the current factual view because their underlying artifacts were not reproduced.

    Scientific release history

    Application 2.0.0 introduced the complete GENCODE v50 MYCN set plus reviewed RefSeq. MYCN-202 has a changed first exon and 3′ end; MYCN-204 and MYCN-205 have extended 3′ ends. Structures, sequences, CDS checks, ORFs and protein properties were regenerated together.

    Historical: July 2026 public viewer used GENCODE v49 for MYCN-201–206, v50 for MYCN-207, and RefSeq for MYCN-XM. Its ORF numbering and inherited experimental interpretations are not current annotations.
